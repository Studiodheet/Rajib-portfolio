Nisba

Nisba is a halal investing education platform. I designed their investment screening tool from zero to shipped product, taking it from discovery workshops to paying users.

Nisba's students finished their training with knowledge but no way to apply it. Checking whether an index fund was Sharia-compliant meant manually researching hundreds of companies, so most people simply didn't. We ran discovery workshops, surveyed their 10,000+ subscribers, and designed a screening tool that connects to Trading 212 and checks the S&P 500 and NASDAQ 100 for halal and BDS compliance.

AI monitors compliance changes and alerts users, but never decides for them; research showed trust depended on that boundary. Within the first week of early access at $4.99/month, the screener had 60 sign-ups before any promotion to the full email list.

BrightLocal

BrightLocal is a local SEO platform used by thousands of agencies. I led a year-long brand and product transformation that reignited stalled revenue.

Revenue had plateaued and the brand was indistinguishable from competitors; even internal teams struggled to tell them apart. Over 12 months I directed a company-wide brand refresh spanning tone of voice, illustration and experiential, alongside a scalable product design system and five core product design principles.

Trial-to-paid conversion rose 28%, monthly revenue from new users grew 36%, and the sales team closed their first enterprise deals. The design team grew from 4 to 16, with design reframed internally as a growth lever rather than a visual layer.

American Express

American Express needed a modern, digital-first identity to close a generational brand gap. My team unified the work of Pentagram, McCann and Ogilvy into one global system.

Global markets were interpreting the brand their own way; one APAC team had even redesigned the logo themselves because the guidelines didn't fit their digital needs. Acting as the connective tissue across the agencies and Amex's senior leadership, I led the development of a modular brand system with fixed pillars and flexible execution.

We codified everything from type hierarchy to motion principles, built a vetted asset library, and travelled across key regions delivering in-person workshops. The system ended years of fragmented execution and remains the backbone of Amex's global brand strategy.

TaylorMade Golf

TaylorMade's app was a basic score tracker. I led its reimagining as a digital clubhouse, pairing player data with coaching, insights and gear recommendations.

The original app served one narrow purpose, manual round tracking, with no reason to open it between rounds. Through stakeholder interviews, benchmarking and user testing, we rebuilt it from the ground up with a layered insight system: glance-level clarity for most golfers, deeper performance breakdowns for those who wanted them, native across iOS, Android and Apple Watch.

Downloads doubled in the first year and sales rose 18.6%, driven by deeper engagement and data-led gear recommendations. One review put it best: like having a coach in your pocket without the price tag.

Made by ISS

ISS is a global facilities provider with 400,000 staff and over 120 UK office restaurants. I consolidated a dozen food brands into one. Made by ISS.

Their food services arm needed to compete with the high street while wrangling a sprawling house of brands. My award-winning answer was to move to a single, familiar, repeatable consumer brand, backed by a centralised online ordering platform for location managers across the global network.

The consolidation enabled a centralised marketing function that saved ISS millions, and a recognisable global brand that has been a key factor in winning new clients.
